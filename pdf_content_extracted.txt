1.1 Our Story
Every great journey begins with a purpose.
Sports Life Tennis Academy was born from a simple belief—that every player deserves access to 
quality coaching, the right opportunities, and an environment that inspires growth.
Our founder's journey in tennis began at the age of four and evolved through years of competitive 
tennis, international coaching experience in Spain, and working with players at different stages of 
development. Along the way, one thing became clear: many talented players had the passion and 
potential to succeed but often lacked access to structured guidance, consistent match play, quality 
facilities, and long-term player development.
Inspired by these experiences, Sports Life Tennis Academy was established in 2024 at Roshanara 
Club with a vision of creating more than just a tennis academy. Our goal was to build an 
organization that places players at the centre of everything it does—where coaching goes beyond 
technique and focuses on developing complete athletes and responsible individuals.
Driven by this vision, Sports Life has continued to grow and now proudly operates across 
Roshanara Club, Major Dhyan Chand Sports Complex, and Punjabi Bagh Club, providing 
players with access to 14 professional synthetic courts, 10 clay courts, and 3 grass courts. More 
importantly, we have built a team of passionate coaches who share the same commitment to 
excellence, integrity, and player-ﬁrst development.
Today, Sports Life is more than a place to learn tennis. It is a growing community where players, 
parents, and coaches come together with a shared purpose—to learn, compete, grow, and contribute 
to the future of Indian tennis.
As we continue to expand, our mission remains unchanged: to create opportunities, inspire dreams, 
and empower every player to achieve their highest potential.
Our story is still being written, and every player who joins Sports Life becomes a part of it.

2.1 Meet the Founder 
⭐
My Journey
Every journey begins with a single moment.
For me, that moment came when I was just 4–5 years old.
My father was a huge cricket fan, and naturally, he wanted me to become a cricketer. I was equally 
excited about playing cricket. One day, my father took me to a cricket academy to enroll me. 
However, the coaches there told him that I was still too young to join.
Both of us were disappointed.
As we were leaving the academy, we noticed a group of players practicing on the nearby tennis 
courts. My father was fascinated by the sport and decided to speak with one of the tennis coaches. 
The coach welcomed us and said that I could start learning tennis immediately.
That unexpected conversation changed my life forever.
What began as an alternative to cricket soon became my greatest passion.
Over the next 16 years, tennis became much more than a sport. It became my teacher, my 
discipline, and my way of life. During my playing career, I competed professionally, won several 
AITA tournaments, represented my college in numerous competitions, and experienced both 
victories and defeats that shaped my character.
In 2016, I travelled to Vilas Tennis Academy in Mallorca, Spain, where I experienced 
international tennis training for the ﬁrst time. That experience completely changed my perspective 
on player development and inspired me to dream bigger.
However, life had different plans.
In 2020, when the COVID-19 pandemic struck, my family faced severe ﬁnancial difﬁculties. 
Continuing my professional tennis journey was no longer possible. One of the hardest decisions of 
my life was stepping away from the sport I loved because we simply could not afford it.
For more than two years, my focus shifted entirely towards supporting my family and rebuilding 
our ﬁnancial stability. Those years taught me resilience, responsibility, and the importance of never 
giving up, even when life becomes uncertain.
Just when I thought my journey had taken a different direction, tennis gave me another opportunity.
In 2023, I received the opportunity to work in Spain at Jofre Porta Academy in Mallorca. Jofre 
Porta, the former coach of Rafael Nadal and Carlos Moyá, welcomed me into his coaching 
environment.

---PAGE 10---
Working alongside him was one of the greatest learning experiences of my career.
I gained valuable insights into player development, technical and tactical coaching, tournament 
scheduling, long-term planning, and building champions through structured systems rather than 
short-term success.
I later continued my learning journey at the J.C. Ferrero Tennis Academy in Villena, Spain, one of 
the world's leading high-performance tennis academies and the training base of Carlos Alcaraz.
During my time in Spain, I met numerous Indian players and parents. Listening to their experiences 
made me realize that talented players often face challenges beyond coaching. High-quality training, 
tournament planning, international exposure, travel, accommodation, ﬁnancial support, and access 
to the right guidance remain signiﬁcant obstacles for many families.
Those conversations changed the way I viewed tennis.
When I returned to India, I had the privilege of working with the Sports Authority of India (SAI) 
under the Khelo India Talent Search and Development Program, where I contributed to the 
development of the induction protocol for tennis players entering the Khelo India Scheme.
That experience strengthened my belief that Indian tennis needs strong systems as much as it needs 
talented players.
With this vision, I founded Sports Life Tennis Academy.
Our objective has never been limited to teaching forehands and backhands.
We wanted to create an environment where players receive everything they need to grow—not only 
technically, but physically, mentally, and personally.
In 2024, Sports Life began its journey at Roshanara Club.
By 2026, we have 
 expanded to Major Dhyan Chand Sports Complex and Punjabi Bagh Club, allowing us to 
impact the lives of many more players across Delhi.
Since our inception, we have continuously looked beyond traditional coaching. We have organized 
exclusive online learning sessions with Jofre Porta, giving our players the opportunity to learn 
directly from one of the world's most respected coaches. We have also connected our players with 
accomplished Indian professionals such as Vishnu Vardhan, helping them gain valuable insights 
from athletes who have competed at the highest level.
These initiatives reﬂect our philosophy that learning should never stop and that players deserve 
access to the very best knowledge available.
Today, Sports Life is more than a tennis academy.
It is a growing community built around one belief:
Every player deserves the opportunity to reach their full potential.
Our vision extends far beyond coaching lessons.

We want to solve the real challenges faced by Indian tennis players by creating opportunities for 
structured match play, tournament support, quality coaching, ﬁtness development, educational

We believe tennis can change lives, just as it changed mine.
